## Evidence
- **Qualitative (M2):** "We keep a WhatsApp group as the real system" (UXR-02)
- **Quantitative (M3):** The critical signal for the drivers is that complete shift handoff takes about 7x longer. This is the primary driver for 48 minutes of daily wasted time. This time gap explains why the drivers skip using the app for recording their shifts. Average workarounds have increased by 3.4x what it was 2 years ago. The heat map indicates that drivers are most dissatisfied with the scheduling feature with an NPS score of 1.9 and this is one of the main scores that drive the NPS collapse. This relates back to the hook that drivers are dissatisfied with the scheduling feature in the app and have increased their workarounds to counteract the time spent over scheduling in the app.

## Persona & problem
- **Role:** the Frontline Driver, a working delivery driver who performs the same core actions dozens of times per shift under real-world physical constraints.
- **Goal:** Complete deliveries quickly and move to the next stop without the app slowing him down.
- **Friction:** Essential, high-frequency actions require too many steps. The misery isn't abstract complexity; it's complexity colliding with a physical moment where he has no spare hand, no spare time, and no patience left.
- **Problem you are solving:** the time taken to record key, high frequency actions against their shift

## Outcome & metrics
- **Strategic outcome:** the AI layer that uses a one-click solution and a live track of their location against their destination would be the solution to the drivers loosing time over recording their shifts and recording high frequency actions as well. This aims to also increase retention by beating the workaround and this would reduce churn by improving the NPS score against the driver's satisfaction with the scheduling feature  and revenue would increase as well by reducing the time lost by reducing the driver's time spent over using the feature.
- **Primary success metric:** a reduction in time on task towards the benchmark of 4.5 min from 11.3 min.
- **Guardrail metric:** the user churn rate for drivers against the complete shift handoff should not drop below the current percentage.
- **Decision window:** 2 full cohorts

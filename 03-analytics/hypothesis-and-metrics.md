# Data-Backed Hypothesis, Module 3

- **Scenario:** RouteLogic Velocity (B2B)
- **Bet type:** Optimizing the existing

## The hypothesis
> Based on the findings that complete shift handoff takes about 48 minutes of the driver's daily wasted time with an NPS score of 1.9 and average workarounds have increased by 3.4x over the past 2 years, we believe that solving the time taken to record key, high frequency actions like marking their package as  "delivered" for the Frontline Driver who performs the same actions dozens of times per shift under real-world physical constraints will result in an  increase in the driver's retention rate by beating the workaround and improve the driver's satisfaction against the scheduling feature, as measured by a reduction in time on task towards the benchmark of 4.5 min from 11.3 min. We will protect the user churn rate for drivers against the complete shift handoff and make a go/no-go decision after 2 full cohorts.

## Evidence
- **Qualitative (M2):** "We keep a WhatsApp group as the real system" (UXR-02)
- **Quantitative (M3):** the findings that complete shift handoff takes about 48 minutes of the driver's daily wasted time with an NPS score of 1.9 and average workarounds have increased by 3.4x over the past 2 years

## Persona & problem
- **Role:** the Frontline Driver who performs the same actions dozens of times per shift under real-world physical constraints
- **Goal:** complete deliveries quickly and move to the next stop without the app slowing him down
- **Friction:** Essential, high-frequency actions require too many steps. The misery isn't abstract complexity; it's complexity colliding with a physical moment where he has no spare hand, no spare time, and no patience left
- **Problem you are solving:** the time taken to record key, high frequency actions like marking their package as  "delivered"

## Outcome & metrics
- **Strategic outcome:** an  increase in the driver's retention rate by beating the workaround and improve the driver's satisfaction against the scheduling feature
- **Primary success metric:** a reduction in time on task towards the benchmark of 4.5 min from 11.3 min
- **Guardrail metric:** the user churn rate for drivers against the complete shift handoff
- **Decision window:** 2 full cohorts

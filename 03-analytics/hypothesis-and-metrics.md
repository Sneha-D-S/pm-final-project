## The hypothesis
> Based on the complete shift handoff taking about 7x longer. This is the primary driver for 48 minutes of daily wasted time. This relates back to the hook that drivers are dissatisfied with the high frequency tasks in the app and have increased their workarounds to counteract the time spent on the app, we believe that solving the time taken to record key, high frequency actions like marking their package as  "delivered" for the Frontline Driver who performs the same actions dozens of times per shift under real-world physical constraints will result in an  increase in the driver's retention rate by beating the workaround and improve the driver's satisfaction against the scheduling feature, as measured by a reduction in time on task towards the benchmark of 4.5 min from 11.3 min. We will protect the user churn rate for drivers against the complete shift handoff and make a go/no-go decision after 2 full cohorts.

## Persona & problem
- **Role:** the Frontline Driver who performs the same actions dozens of times per shift under real-world physical constraints
- **Goal:** complete deliveries quickly and move to the next stop without the app slowing him down
- **Friction:** Essential, high-frequency actions require too many steps. The misery isn't abstract complexity; it's complexity colliding with a physical moment where he has no spare hand, no spare time, and no patience left
- **Problem you are solving:** the time taken to record key, high frequency actions like marking their package as  "delivered"

## Outcome & metrics
- **Strategic outcome:** an  increase in the driver's retention rate by beating the workaround and improve the driver's satisfaction against the scheduling feature
- **Primary success metric:** a reduction in time on task towards the benchmark of 4.5 min from 11.3 min
- **Guardrail metric:** the user churn rate for drivers against the complete shift handoff
- **Decision window:** 2 full cohorts

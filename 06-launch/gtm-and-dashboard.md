# GTM Launch Plan, RouteLogic (B2B)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Shift Handoff Wizard, |
| Goal | Engagement |
| Launch tier | M, Targeted |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Engagement, The feature is already well known in the market. The issue is that existing users are abandoning RouteLogic for competitors.
- **Target audience:** Primary: Fleet coordinators and delivery drivers. This is because the coordinators will ensure that the drivers adopt the new feature only if they find it useful as a source of truth for tracking the driver's shifts and delivery status. 
Secondary: The drivers need to be convinced that the Shift Handoff Wizard is easy for them to use so that they do not resist the adoption when the coordinators tell them to use it.

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, An enhancement to an existing feature within a competitive market with an established user base. The launch requires communication across existing customers to convince them that the new feature is better than the competition.  A full-scale XL campaign isn’t yet warranted, but a targeted focused communication approach is essential to build awareness and drive user trust and retention.

## Channels
1. **Owned: In app "feature update" alert on the landing screen, Emails and app push notifications.**
2. **Earned: Testimonials of the new feature on the website.**
3. **Paid: Advertisements on Route-logic's Youtube and Facebook with Videos of drivers and fleet coordinators using the new feature and endorsing it.**

## Enablement & Assets
Launch email, in-app short gif to spread awareness of the feature, in-app push notifications, existing user testimonials, recruitment of users to participate in the video endorsement series.

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** PM: Owns the launch plan, coordinates all teams, and monitors post-launch metrics, Marketing: Owns user outreach, paid-social creative, and the launch email. Budget required for paid-social activation and user endorsers fees, Designer: Owns the demo video, social visuals, and app-store assets. Engineer: Owns app-store submission and performance monitoring on launch day. Customer Support: Briefed on feature coaching logic before launch. No additional budget required.
- **Timeline:** Week 1: Upcoming "New feature" announcement in app and email anouncement.
Week 2: In app gif for upcoming feature tutorial and awareness goes live. Small group of early users are identified. 
Week 3: Launch day, paid social activates and the launch email goes out.
Week 4 onwards: Monitor metrics, collect feedback, and assess the iteration decision.

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Feature adoption  in the first 30 days: measures whether the awareness goal is driving existing users to start using this feature.
Time to first key action: How quickly users can "Clock-in" when their shift starts, how quickly the users can mark "Delivered" after the package has been delivered and how quickly users can "Clock-out" at the end of their shift. 
Feature adoption rate: the % of users who complete logging their complete shift and delivery status within their first week.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** High-reach but no feature adoption, Users still using external tools and competitors for marking their shift.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** double down on the advertisements and focus on usability testing and iteration to improve user satisfaction and promote trust
